Symphonia Legato

Music notation software, for people who read music but shouldn't need an engineering degree to write it down.

✓ Builds, tests, and runs today (2026-09-15)

The problem it solves

Writing down music by hand or in generic drawing tools is slow and error-prone. Commercial notation software (Encore, Finale, Sibelius) is expensive, often subscription-based, and closed-source. Symphonia Legato is a free, open-source alternative: click or type to enter notes, hear them played back instantly, and export a professional-looking score as sheet music (PDF) or an industry-standard file (MusicXML) that other software can open.

Key benefits

Free & open-source
MIT licensed — no subscription, no vendor lock-in, source code available to inspect or extend.
Hear what you write
Every note plays back immediately, with a metronome and count-in, so mistakes are caught by ear, not just by eye.
Works with other software
MusicXML and MIDI import/export mean scores aren't trapped in one app.
Cross-platform
Runs on Windows, Linux, and macOS, with a companion Android app for viewing and playing scores on the go.
Built-in AI assistance
Optional chord detection, fingering suggestions, and (with a Claude API key) harmonisation and practice recommendations.
Accessible by design
A dedicated high-contrast theme and screen-reader labels on every interactive control.
