SymphoniaLegato.Android

Executive Overview — your scores, in your pocket

What it does, in plain terms

A companion app for viewing and playing back scores on a phone or tablet — useful for practicing away from the desktop computer where the score was written. It's a viewer today, not a full editor (see the project's docs/TODO.md for editing-parity plans).

Where it sits

Core, NotationEngine, PlaybackEngine, LayoutEngine, ImportExport, Rendering
→
Android

Why it matters

  • Practice doesn't have to happen at a desk — the score and playback travel with the musician.
  • Shares the exact same rendering as Desktop, so nothing looks different on the smaller screen.
