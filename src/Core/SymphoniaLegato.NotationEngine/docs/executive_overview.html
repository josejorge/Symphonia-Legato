SymphoniaLegato.NotationEngine

Executive Overview — the "editing" in music notation editor

What it does, in plain terms

Every time you click a note, delete one, or press Ctrl+Z, this is the piece that makes it happen — and makes it reversible. It also automatically fixes up the visual details a musician expects after any edit: which notes get beamed together, which get a sharp or flat symbol shown.

Why it matters

  • Undo/redo works for every kind of edit, not just some — because everything goes through one command pipeline.
  • You never have to manually re-flag accidentals or fix beaming after an edit; it happens automatically.
