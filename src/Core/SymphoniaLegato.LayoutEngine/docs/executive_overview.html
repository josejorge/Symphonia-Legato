SymphoniaLegato.LayoutEngine

Executive Overview — where notes become a page of sheet music

What it does, in plain terms

Decides where every note, bar line, and staff actually goes on the page — how many measures fit per line, when to wrap to a new line, how big the page is. This is the difference between "a list of notes" and "something that looks like real sheet music."

Why it matters

  • Automatic layout means a musician never has to manually fight with spacing or line breaks.
  • The same layout feeds both what you see on screen and what gets exported to PDF/PNG/SVG, so they always match.
