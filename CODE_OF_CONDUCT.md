# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Symphonia Legato a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
